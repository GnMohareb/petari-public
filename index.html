Petari helps you organize pet profiles, care notes, schedules and attachments, and find nearby veterinary clinics.

The first Android release is being prepared. This page provides support and privacy information; it does not offer an app download yet.

Support and privacy enquiries

Contact gmohareb@gitinc.ca. Include your app version, phone model and a description of the issue. Do not send passwords or identity documents.

Your records stay on your phone

The local-only release needs no Petari account and contains no ads. Clinic search and maps use public internet providers. Read the privacy policy for details.

Export or delete local records

In Petari, open Passport, the account/privacy icon, then Device storage & privacy. You can export records or delete local records there. Save attachment copies separately; import and restore are not available in this version.

Uninstalling Petari or clearing its storage can permanently lose records. Exported copies must be managed wherever you saved them.
